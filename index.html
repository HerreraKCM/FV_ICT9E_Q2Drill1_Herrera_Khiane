⛸️ First Glides: A Beginner’s Roadmap to Ice Skating



Figure skating is a sport where athletes perform routines on ice, combining jumps, spins and footwork with music. It is known for blending athletic skill with artistic expression.



📜 Basic Rules

1. ISU Regulations: Figure skating competitions include various event formats and a specific competition structure governed by the ISU.

2. Costume requirements: Men are required to wear trousers, while women are required to wear tights, trousers, or unitards, often with skirts.

3. Protests: There are established protest procedures in place for competitions.

4. Safety guidelines: Skaters must adhere to safety guidelines, including wearing appropriate protective gear and following proper training protocols.
More Rules! ISU Rules!
☃️ Equipment

1. Ice skates that fit properly and provide ankle support

2. Warm clothing, including gloves, beanie/hat, leg warmers and layers to stay comfortable in cold temperatures.

3. A helmet, knee pads, and wrist guards for safety

4. A skating aid or support for beginners, such as a walker or a chair

5. A water bottle to stay hydrated during practice.
Additional Equipments!
❄️ Techniques

1. Start with a proper stance, keeping your knees slightly bent and your weight centered

2. Practice gliding on one foot and then the other to improve balance

3. Learn to stop by using the snowplow or T-stop technique

4. Practice turning by leaning your body in the direction you want to go

5. Gradually increase your speed and try more advanced techniques, such as crossovers.
